Assembly
  1. Print the coil former — PLA at 0.2 mm layer height, 20% infill. Orient vertically for best cylindrical accuracy.
  2. Wind the coil — Feed AWG 24 magnet wire through the channel. Wind 100 turns per layer, 10 layers, keeping tension even.
  3. Secure the winding — Apply polyurethane varnish or wrap with Kapton tape to prevent unravelling.
  4. Add tuning capacitor — Connect ~1077 µF in parallel across the coil terminals. Use a film or electrolytic cap rated for low-frequency AC.
  5. Connect preamp — Feed into a low-noise instrumentation amplifier (gain ~60 dB) with a 1–20 Hz band-pass filter.
  6. Orient and measure — Point the loop axis toward the signal source. For Schumann resonances, orient vertically with the axis pointing N–S.
Transmit efficiency is ~10−23. At 7 Hz, practical transmission requires kilometre-scale ground electrodes (as used by military ELF stations). This coil is designed as a receiver for natural ELF phenomena — Schumann resonances, sferic signatures, and geomagnetic micropulsations.
